## What It Does

- **Each service's image:** for a Google shared drive, a Google form and
  others, the exact size, where the service cuts the image and what shows,
  with an example.
- **Measured from the service itself:** a grid of labeled cells from
  PlaceMellon, uploaded at the size the service asks for, shows which parts
  survive the crop.
- **A service it doesn't cover yet?** Measure it yourself the same way.

## Who It's For

Anyone setting up a shared drive, a form or another service for a CMU group.
It's named for the Cut, the lawn at the center of campus, and a cut list,
the list of exact sizes to cut.
