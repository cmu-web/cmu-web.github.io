## What It Does

- **Any size in the address:** `https://placemellon.andrew.cmu.edu/400/300`
  is a 400 by 300 campus photo, cropped to fit.
- **Photos or graphics:** campus, classrooms, labs or campus life, in color or
  grayscale, plus Scotty, a bio placeholder and solid boxes for wireframes.
- **A builder** to preview the result and copy its address or HTML.
- **Production assets,** like the sample ID card and default profile
  picture, that are fine on live sites.

## Who It's For

Anyone mocking up a CMU page or app. For a finished page, swap the placeholder photo for the original from the source collection.
