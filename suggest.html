Suggest a Service

Have an idea for a tool that would help Carnegie Mellon websites and web apps? Tell us what you'd build and who it would help.

What We Build

CMU Web's services cover the pieces around a site: components and conventions, a theme for GitHub Pages, placeholder images, QR codes and link previews. A good idea saves time for more than one office or team, and works for anyone at CMU without an account to set up.

Tell Us About Your Idea

Include a few details so we can understand it:

  • What the service would do.
  • Who would use it, and what they do today instead.
  • Any examples, links or screenshots that show the problem.

Suggest a Service

To report a problem with one of the existing services, open an issue in its GitHub repository instead.
