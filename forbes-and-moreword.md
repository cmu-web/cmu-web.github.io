## What It Does

- **Write in the block editor, get cmu.edu.** Every page gets CMU's roof, the
  site's name, tabs for its pages and the footer.
- **The Web Kilt's components as patterns:** FAQ, Text Section, Cards on Gray,
  Leadership Team, Callout and Numbered Steps, under Carnegie Mellon Web Kilt
  in the inserter.
- **News that looks like CMU news:** posts open with the Kilt's Article, and
  Latest Posts becomes the template's News Grid.
- **Install like any theme:** upload the release's zip under Appearance ›
  Themes, then activate it. WordPress 6.6 or later.

## Who It's For

Anyone running a WordPress site for a CMU office, program, lab or event. It's
named for the corner of Forbes and Morewood. Same intersection, more words.
